My favorite places to see in Egypt

1. The Pyramids of Giza

The Great Pyramid of Khufu: The largest and oldest of the three, built for Pharaoh Khufu with an original height of 146.5 meters. The Pyramid of Khafre: Built for Khufu's son, Khafre, it appears taller from certain angles because it is set on a higher elevation and retains some of its original smooth limestone casing stones at the top. The Pyramid of Menkaure: The smallest of the three main structures, built for Khufu's grandson, Menkaure, featuring a striking red granite base

2. Khan El-Khalili

Khan El-Khalili is a historic market in Cairo, known for its traditional architecture and vibrant atmosphere. It's a great place to experience the local culture and shop for souvenirs.
